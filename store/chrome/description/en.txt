Kick Chat Translator is a free live chat translator for Kick. Read any stream's chat in your own language, and reply in the channel's language, right inside the chat.

Watching a Turkish, Spanish, Russian or Arabic stream and can't follow the chat? Install, open the stream, and every message shows its translation right under it as it arrives. No account, no API key, nothing to set up.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Open any Kick stream. A green bar at the top of the chat shows the translator is on.
2. Each new message gets its translation underneath, in your browser's language.
3. Type a reply in your own language. A preview shows it in the channel's language: press Tab or click it, then send.

The channel's language is read from Kick itself, and both languages can be changed in settings. Works on live streams and on VOD replays, keeps 7TV emotes as they are, and pauses itself in background tabs.

WHAT YOU GET

• 43 languages, right to left included (Arabic, Hebrew, Persian), with regional variants kept apart (Brazilian Portuguese, Traditional Chinese).
• Free out of the box with Google Translate, no key and no account.
• Better European languages with your own DeepL API key. DeepL gets the recent chat lines as context, so the wording fits the conversation.
• On-device translation in Chrome and Edge 138+ when your computer supports it: offline, unlimited, and the chat never leaves your machine.
• Automatic fallback: when one engine fails, MyMemory or Lingva takes over. You choose the order.
• Four display styles: under the message, on the same line, in small italics after it, or only when you hover. Hover mode translates nothing until you point at a message, which cuts usage about ten times on a fast chat.
• Filters: skip bots, mute users or channels, hide lines that contain a word you choose (handy against !command spam), or translate only the languages you want.
• Emotes, mentions, links and emoji spam are stripped before anything is sent, so nothing is spent translating "kkkkkk".
• Make it yours: text size, line spacing, typeface, accent colour, dark or light chat, a reading language remembered per channel, keyboard shortcuts.
• Interface in 10 languages: English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Turkish, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

PRIVACY

No account, no analytics, no server of mine. Chat text goes only to the translation engine you picked. With Google, MyMemory or Lingva, that is the text of each message and nothing else. With DeepL, the last few chat lines go along as context, with the names of whoever wrote them, so the translation fits the conversation. The channel you watch is never sent, and with on-device translation nothing leaves your computer.

The permissions, in plain words: storage for your settings and its translation cache; access to kick.com to show translations in the chat; access to the engines it can call (Google, DeepL, MyMemory and Lingva); a wake-up timer so the translator stays ready while you watch; and the right to reload its own script into Kick tabs already open when you install or update it, so they keep translating without a refresh. It also reads the GitHub releases page to tell you when a newer version exists, sending nothing with that request.

WHAT'S NEW

Chat abbreviations like ngl, idc, lmk, wyd and goat are spelled out before translation, so readers in other languages get what they mean instead of the letters as typed or a wrong guess.

Common chat words the translator got wrong are now translated from the extension's own table, in more languages: valeu no longer comes back as "it cost", nor yatta as "on the yacht", and greetings like merci, gracias or konnichiwa are translated into Hebrew, Persian, Greek, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Bengali, Tamil, Traditional Chinese and Cantonese.

On a fast chat, lines written in another language than the rest of a batch came back untranslated and were never shown. Each line is now detected and translated on its own.

Fewer lines lost or misread: a short Italian or other foreign line is no longer taken for English and hidden, ordinary words that end like an emote name (Romanian felul, Turkish erkek) are no longer deleted before translation, Arabic greetings are read as Arabic and not Persian, and a reply keeps its @mention and link in Cantonese.

Traditional Chinese translated on your device now comes back in traditional characters.

Lighter on your browser: the extension stops waking up when no Kick tab is open, and costs less per message on a busy chat.

Firefox: after an update, a tab could run two copies of the extension and translate every message twice. Only one runs now.
